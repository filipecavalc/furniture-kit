# Industrial table

Example project: furniture with metal profiles and solid wood.

## Briefing
- Furniture type: industrial dining table
- Size: 1600 x 800 x 760 mm
- Materials and finishes: steel tube frame (50x50x2 legs, 30x50x1.5 frame), matte black paint;
  top of 4 freijo boards 35 mm glued, matte varnish
- Hardware / components: 4 levelling feet
- Who builds it: metal shop (frame) + cabinet shop (top)

## Decisions
- Example created to validate the library (profiles, solid wood, bar cut list, render).

## Open items
- Stock bar length (6000 mm) and saw kerf (3 mm): confirm with the metal shop.
- How the top is fixed to the frame.
- Real freijo tone (the render is an approximation).

## Deliverables
- `cutlist/`: bill of materials (CSV/MD) and bar cut list
- `drawings/`: assembly drawing with automatic overall dimensions
- `images/`: standard views and exploded view
- `render/`: photo-style render
